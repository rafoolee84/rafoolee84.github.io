Snap Purge AI

Clear the screenshots clogging your iPhone.

How it works

1. It finds your screenshots

Only screenshots. Your camera photos are never touched. The text in each one is read on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework.

2. You pick what goes

Review one at a time, or select a whole pile. You'll see an estimate of the space before you delete.

3. iOS confirms, and you can undo

iOS asks before anything is deleted. Deleted screenshots stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Empty it to get the space back right away.

Questions

Does it delete my photos?

Version 1.0 only looks at screenshots. Nothing is deleted until you choose it and iOS confirms.

Can I get something back?

Yes, for 30 days: Photos > Albums > Recently Deleted.

Why "AI"?

It reads the text in your screenshots with Apple's on-device machine-learning text recognition (Vision). There's no chatbot and no cloud AI.

Does it find duplicate photos?

Not in version 1.0. It's a screenshot cleaner.
