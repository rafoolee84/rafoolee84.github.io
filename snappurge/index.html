SnapPurge

Screenshot cleaner for iPhone

SnapPurge reads the text in your screenshots on your iPhone and sorts the temporary ones into piles: receipts, one-time codes, Wi-Fi and tracking shots, and anything older than 30 days. Review them, then clear what you don't need. Nothing is uploaded.

Common questions

  • How do I cancel? Settings > your name > Subscriptions > SnapPurge. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal or before the trial ends.
  • I bought Pro on another iPhone. Open SnapPurge, tap the badge at the top right, then Restore Purchases.
  • Can I get a deleted screenshot back? Yes, for 30 days: Photos > Albums > Recently Deleted.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
